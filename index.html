Intentaro Android privacy policy

Effective: 18 October 2026
Developer: Tech Solutions
Privacy and support contact: techsolution4512@gmail.com

This policy covers the Intentaro Android app. The separate laptop app keeps its own local browser storage.

Data on your phone

Intentaro stores the habits, goals, schedules, progress entries, focus intentions, confirmed sessions, reminder settings, saved focus routines, chosen app identifiers, and permission-consent choices you create. These are used to display your progress, run timers, schedule reminders, and apply the optional focus shield. By default, your information stays in Android's private app storage. Intentaro has no account, app server, cloud syncing, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or internet permission. The app does not upload or sell your habit or app-activity data.

Local progress insights

Focus totals, daily charts, session outcomes, and habit consistency are calculated on your phone from saved sessions and habit entries. These insights do not monitor other apps, collect screen-time activity, or send progress to an analytics service. Undone and unconfirmed sessions do not contribute to focus totals. Older records without daily timing detail are shown separately rather than assigned invented daily activity.

Optional manual file transfer

If you choose Export Android data, Intentaro writes a readable, unencrypted transfer file to the location you select using Android's file picker. The app requests local locations such as phone storage or USB. The file includes habits, goals, progress, confirmed focus records, reminders, routines, blocking schedule names/times/days, and quiet hours. Blocking schedules transfer turned off with empty app lists and cleared emergency skips. Selected app identifiers, blocking consent, and unfinished sessions are excluded. Keep the file private. Import validates the file and shows a preview before you confirm replacement of this app's data; records are not merged. Imported reminders are off, snoozes cleared, and blocking requires setup again. No Android permission is granted by an import. Intentaro does not keep permanent access to a selected file or automatically create or synchronize transfer files. Laptop transfer files are not supported by the Android importer.

Optional accessibility access

If you agree to the separate disclosure and enable the focus shield, Android Accessibility provides foreground app identifiers. Intentaro compares those identifiers with the apps you selected and shows a focus screen during your protected session or a blocking schedule you explicitly enable, including while Intentaro is not on screen. Schedules automatically cover their own chosen apps at your selected times and repeat days; they never start a focus timer or award progress. Schedule settings and the start date of an emergency-skipped occurrence are saved locally. Ending current scheduled lockouts skips all overlapping occurrences until their respective ends. Future occurrences remain enabled. Pausing a manual timer does not pause separate schedules. It does not retrieve screen text, passwords, messages, or website content. Foreground app activity is processed temporarily and is not kept as a usage log. Selected app identifiers are saved locally. You may disable accessibility access in Android Settings or withdraw blocking consent in the app. Withdrawing consent turns blocking schedules off. Disabling Android accessibility access stops blocking; reconnecting restores schedules that remain enabled. Phone, Settings, and uninstall controls remain available. Schedule checks run in the connected accessibility service while the screen is awake and on app switches/unlock. No screen content is retrieved, no wakeup alarm or foreground timer is started for schedules, and Android or device controls can delay or stop enforcement.

Notifications and timing

Notification permission is optional and is used for timer status and reminders. Notifications can display reminder titles, routine names, or focus intentions on your lock screen, depending on your Android notification settings. Scheduled focus routines send an invitation to review and start; they do not automatically start a timer or app blocking. Optional Alarms & reminders access improves reminder timing. Android receives the scheduling instructions needed to deliver local notifications. These permissions do not send your progress to Intentaro or an analytics provider.

Retention and deletion

Your saved data remains on your phone until you undo entries, remove reminders or blocking schedules, replace data through a confirmed import, clear app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Archiving a habit retains its history. Clearing storage and uninstalling erase this app's private data; Intentaro cannot recover it without a valid transfer file you previously created. Exported files remain in their chosen location after uninstall and must be deleted separately using your file manager. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. There is no account or server copy to delete.

Device protection

Android's app sandbox protects local storage; the app does not provide end-to-end encryption or a separate app password. Someone using your unlocked phone may see your information. Use your phone's screen lock and notification privacy controls. Device compromise can undermine local protections.

External services and support

Google Play and Android may process information under their own policies when you obtain the app, manage permissions, or use platform diagnostics. Intentaro does not embed analytics or crash-upload SDKs. Opening the project page launches your browser, whose own privacy settings apply. If you choose to contact the project publicly, share only information you intend to make public and do not include private habit content.
